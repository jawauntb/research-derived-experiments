## Question

What is the narrowest defensible contribution at the intersection of
Information-Limited Discovery, bounded action admission, active experiment
selection, and prior Concern-Gated Retrieval work?

## Perspective scan

### Practitioner

The four-status interface is operationally useful because it prevents an agent
from conflating “cannot know,” “cannot afford,” and “already know.” Exact
planning is limited to small projections, so production value depends on
honest abstraction and caching.

### Academic

The recurrence belongs to optimal decision-tree and adaptive experimental
design lineages. The defensible contribution is a target-relative,
certificate-bearing benchmark and integration contract, not new optimization
mathematics.

### Skeptic

The exhaustive benchmark is synthetic and hand-bounded. Its failure rates
cannot be extrapolated to science, coding agents, or retrieval. A learned agent
could fail before the controller by proposing the wrong worlds or target.

### Incentives

Calling the result a universal theory of agency would increase apparent
ambition while making the novelty and validity claims false. A narrower exact
contract is easier to audit, reproduce, and extend.

### Historian

The structure resembles optimal testing, active learning, rational
metareasoning, CEGAR, and query learning. The distinct trajectory in this
repository is the preservation of failed mechanisms as regression constraints.

## Contradiction map

1. **Greedy target ambiguity is principled versus greedy is optimal.** Both can
   be separated: target awareness is useful, but exact counterexamples show the
   immediate score is myopic.
2. **Admission is a universal structure versus admission is a finite control
   abstraction.** Current evidence supports only the latter.
3. **Concern should guide selection versus concern geometry failed.** Concern
   may remain a soft nominator; it cannot be a hard scientific verifier on the
   existing evidence.
4. **Exact planning is normative versus exact planning is scalable.** The
   first holds on the declared finite system; the second is unsupported.

## Synthesis

The clean one-shot contribution is to make the admission boundary executable:
use exact continuation cost as the small-instance control oracle, preserve a
terminal target-distinct pair as the structural impossibility certificate, and
give resource insufficiency its own status. The smallest counterexample to the
old greedy rule then becomes a permanent regression rather than an abandoned
negative result.

## Reliability

- Exact finite recurrence and soundness: 10/10 inside assumptions.
- Exhaustive registered counts: 10/10, replayable deterministic receipt.
- Greedy limitation inside the boundary: 10/10.
- Methodological novelty: 6/10; integration is plausible, but prior-art search
  cannot prove global novelty.
- Natural-agent relevance: 3/10 until a held-out empirical adapter is run.
