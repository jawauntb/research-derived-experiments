# Expressivity is not access, and access is process-relative

**Jawaun Brown** (human director) and **Cursor Grok 4.6** (agent, under review)
**Date:** August 17, 2026
**Status:** Bounded methods/results synthesis. Ready as a paper of
that size. Not a claim that we built a better language model.

## Current frame

Odrzywołek showed that one binary operator inhabits the scientific
calculator class (arXiv:2603.21852). Completeness there is fiber
inhabitation: `q⁻¹(z) ≠ ∅`. That is not discovery. SIC is the
reason nobody sane stops at inhabitation. The invariance theorem
lives at sharing / DAG / program level. Formula trees have no
invariance theorem. Under a Gibbs prior, discovery probability is
fiber mass `P(z)=Φ_z/Z`, not shortest length.

The protected assumption, after the first EML toys, was that "access
is `Φ`" is a law of search, and that delete–repair is one arrow
that also explains relativity and attention.

## How this relates to better LLMs

It does not relate by sprinkling `eml` into a transformer.

If the frame is right, a better model is one that does four
concrete things:

1. **Search at sharing level, not formula unrolling.** The `sq`
   witness: adjoining a definable macro drops formula size for
   `x^(2^n)` from `2^{n+1}-1` to `n+1` while circuit size stays
   `Θ(n)`. An LLM that expands the same subroutine in every
   residual stream is paying the formula tax. Weight tying, named
   tools, and recurrent reuse are the compiler move. `eml` itself
   is not the architecture.

2. **Do not delete working distinctions.** Content-only attention
   is over-invariance: it quotients away order that the task still
   uses. Paper B's over-toy is that cell. Restore the distinction
   (positions, names, roles). Do not call that "more symmetry."

3. **Do not leave an unearned privileged coordinate.** A leftover
   absolute frame is under-invariance. The identity still
   represents; it is just more expensive. Quotient the privilege
   or add transport. Do not call that "more features."

4. **Do not install one search law.** `Φ` is not "how learning
   works." Gibbs sampling feels `Φ`. Known-tree GD barely feels
   `Φ`. Unknown-tree GD does not. Frozen-leaf rewrite feels `Φ`
   again. A trainer that can retune every constant is a different
   process from a trainer that must keep named structure.

"Discover all" splits into three jobs that this program refuses to
smash:

| Job | Object | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Write all | completeness / inhabitation | Odrzywołek's; not ours |
| Reach all | access / process-relative mass | this synthesis |
| Care which matter | concern / valence | **not done** |

A better LLM is a machine that can *reach* the programs that
matter, without deleting the distinctions those programs use, and
without a fake privileged coordinate. Completeness is cheap once
the substrate exists. Access is the bottleneck. Concern is a
later paper and is not hidden inside `e^x − ln y`.

## Candidate reframe

Access is process-relative. Fiber mass is the right object for a
sampler of known inhabitants. It is not automatically the right
object for an optimizer that can retarget a tree, and it can
become the right object again when the tree's leaves are frozen.
Delete–repair is a real operator with two directions, not a slogan
that smashes Einstein and content-only attention into one arrow.

## Severe experiments: were they adversarial enough?

Yes when they could have gone the other way. No when they only
restated a control.

**Adversarial enough**

- Unknown-skeleton GD was built to *transfer* `Φ`. It tied. We
  banked the rejection instead of dressing 7=7 as a win.
- Frozen-leaf ranking was forced onto *extra* basins. Exact 2 vs 1
  cannot fake `phi_holds`. Equal extras would have rejected. Thin
  extras winning would have killed.
- Paper B's kill was predeclared: crossed success, or one
  minimal-safe screen. A kill still passes CI.

**Not adversarial enough, and labeled as such**

- Paper B does not enumerate a new cube. It pairs Paper A's
  matrix. Anyone who wanted a new world did not get one. The
  value is the kill contract, not new bits.
- Gibbs 2 vs 1 is a control, not a headline, once you refuse to
  sample the census and call that "discovery."
- Matching-skeleton GD's margin is two seeds. That is a fact, not
  a law.
- No experiment here is a trained language model. Shortcut
  learning, prompt artifacts, and benchmark leakage are not the
  failure mode. The failure mode is overclaim.

**Still missing, therefore withheld**

- Neural bootstrap (Odrzywołek's actual search).
- Paper E is banked as a typed miss (`surgery_killed`).
- Paper F is banked: the written κ is SIC (`calculus_is_sic`).
- Valence / concern. Not in `eml`.
- A better LLM. Not shipped.

## Claim boundary

**Supported, methods/results paper.**

Expressivity ≠ tree size ≠ circuit size ≠ access. Access is
process-relative on the registered size-3 EML pair. Delete–repair
has two directions on the registered `{0,1}^4` pair.

**Not supported.**

We discovered all functions. We built a better LLM. `Φ` is how
gradient descent works. Lorentz geometry is a positional encoding.
Paper 0 (`Complex.log 0`) is on this path. OpenAI 2026 writeups
are theorems.

**What would change the conclusion.**

A later process that is neither GD-with-free-leaves nor
frozen-leaf rewrite and still refuses to split; a harness where
the Paper B crossed cell represents; a sharing-level search that
closes the `sq` gap without named macros.
