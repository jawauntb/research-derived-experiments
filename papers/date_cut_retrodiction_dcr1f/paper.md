**Status:** Overall **NO_GO**. R5 (T1 silent at 1880 deep placebo) failed. Per preregistration, this licenses the strong Reading B — but the specific failure mode surfaces something the earlier papers cannot support: **T1 is not a discrete commitment. Any classical field theory presupposes it.**
**Date:** 2026-07-27

## Abstract

DCR1e's finding was that the presupposition-inferring extractor surfaced
T1 content in five documents and `target_v3` rejected all five. DCR1f is
the follow-up preregistered in that paper: a matcher successor (`target_v4`)
widened for presuppositional phrasings, validated against a **held-out set
of scientific-philosophical prose about simultaneity** generated by two
sandboxed subagents blind to the DCR1e outputs and the target_v3 patterns.

**Six gates, six preregistered thresholds, six outcomes:**

| gate | | |
|---|---|---|
| R1 held-out well-formed | GO | 40 T1-positive + 40 T1-negative sentences (both ≥30) |
| R2 T1-positive accuracy | **NO_GO** | 32.5% (13/40) vs 70% threshold |
| R3 T1-negative rejection | GO | 87.5% (35/40) vs 85% threshold |
| R4 T1 fires at 1897 | GO | 2 T1 hits at the near-placebo cut |
| R5 T1 silent at 1880 | **NO_GO** | 1 T1 hit at the deep placebo cut (Maxwell 1865) |
| R6 Newton sanity | GO | 3 T1 hits on Newton, up from DCR1d's 2 |

**Overall NO_GO.** Per DCR1e's preregistered decision table, R5 NO_GO
licenses **Reading B — the extractor is projecting.** DCR1f honors the
preregistration and reports Reading B as the licensed conclusion.

But the specific R5 hit — Maxwell 1865 pt1 *"There is an instant at which
the amount of energy in the whole medium is a definite quantity equally
divided"* — is not obviously projection. It is a real presupposition of
Maxwell's field theory: energy division "at an instant" across a spatially
extended field presupposes that "an instant" is well-defined across
separated points. Read strictly, that is T1 in the electrodynamics of 1865
— which would mean the 1880 cut was never a valid placebo for T1.

The two readings — extractor projected, or placebo was invalid — are not
distinguishable by DCR1f. What DCR1f does establish, and what neither
DCR1c nor DCR1d nor DCR1e could see, is that **T1 is not a discrete
commitment.** It is a spectrum: present as an explicit metaphysical
assertion in Newton 1687, present as a mathematical operation in Larmor
1900's `t → t − vx/c²`, present as a "definite and motion-independent"
duration in Lodge 1897, present as a field-theoretic "instant at which
the whole medium" in Maxwell 1865, present as a "regarded as
simultaneous" convention in Poincaré 1898. **Einstein's deletion was not
one thing. It was a class.** DCR1c's binary "T1 present at cut vs absent
at cut" question underspecified what T1 is.

The programmatic consequence is stated in §7.

- A held-out validation set generated by **two independent sandboxed
  subagents** with no repository access, whose union
  (`heldout_simultaneity_validation.json`, SHA-256
  `8acdee3b9a4ede612a57a9efb37449337db38e889b2124f86992b5b1e49dc003`)

None of that was changed after results came in.

## 2. The strict verdict

**R2 (T1-positive accuracy on held-out set): NO_GO — 32.5%.**

`target_v4` fires on 13 of 40 held-out T1-positive sentences. The 27
misses reveal a specific calibration gap: the sentences use nominalisations
of time and independence in constructions the pattern's spacing windows
and vocabulary lists do not span. Selected representative misses:

> "The duration between two events is a quantity intrinsic to those events,
> and cannot depend upon the accidental circumstances of the observer who
> measures it."
>
> "Two occurrences separated by a wide tract of space nevertheless possess
> a determinate order in time, and this order is common to every rational
> observer."
>
> "The clocks of Greenwich and of Paris, once properly regulated, indicate
> the same instant, and this coincidence is a fact of nature, not a
> stipulation of art."
>
> "The interval elapsing between the striking of the hour at Paris and its
> striking at Berlin is a fact of the physical world, not a construction
> dependent upon the traveller."

Each is unambiguously an assertion of absolute simultaneity. Each uses
period vocabulary (`quantity intrinsic`, `witness`, `spectator`,
`traveller`, `coincidence`, `interval elapsing`) that `target_v4`'s
patterns do not encode. The failure is a matcher-calibration failure, not
a semantic failure.

**R3 (T1-negative rejection): GO — 87.5%.**

`target_v4`'s polarity veto rejects 35 of 40 relativity-of-simultaneity
sentences. The five false positives all use subtle denial constructions
(`phantom`, `unwarranted extrapolation`, `matter of definition`) not
covered by the current veto. Just above threshold; a real defect.

**R4 (T1 fires at 1897 cut): GO — 2 hits.**

The near-placebo cut, whose documents include Michelson 1881,
Michelson-Morley 1887, FitzGerald 1889, Larmor 1897, and Lodge 1897,
produces two T1 hits under `target_v4`: Maxwell 1865 pt1's `instant
across whole medium` (in scope because 1865 ≤ 1897) and Lodge 1897's
`time of journey perfectly definite`. These are exactly the presuppositions
DCR1e surfaced and target_v3 missed.

**R5 (T1 silent at 1880 cut): NO_GO — 1 hit.**

The deep placebo cut, whose documents include only Maxwell 1865 ×2 and
Maxwell 1878, produces one T1 hit under `target_v4`: Maxwell 1865 pt1's
*"There is an instant at which the amount of energy in the whole medium
is a definite quantity equally divided."*

**R6 (Newton sanity): GO — 3 hits**, up from DCR1d's 2. `target_v4`
catches an additional Newton assertion (*"The duration of the existence
of things remains the same whether motions are swift, slow, or none at
all"*) that target_v3 missed.

## 3. The R5 hit and what it forces

The preregistration was explicit: *"If R5 fails, do not remove the 1880
hits by tightening the matcher. That would be exactly the manipulation the
placebo is there to detect. Report R5 NO_GO and license Reading B honestly."*

I am honoring that. The licensed reading is Reading B: the extractor is
projecting T1 content onto Maxwell 1865, and by extension the T1 firings
at 1897 and 1904 cannot be presumed unprojected either.

**But the specific hit does not read like a projection artifact.** The
proposition is not a modern paraphrase supplied by the extractor. It is a
faithful summary of Maxwell's own reasoning in a passage where he argues
that at any given instant the electromagnetic energy of the medium is
equally partitioned between kinetic and potential forms. The word
`instant` in Maxwell's text is doing exactly what an "at the same time
for all points" T1-content proposition says it does: it presupposes that
"an instant" is well-defined across a spatially extended field.

So Reading B, taken at face value, is telling me one of two things I have
not been able to distinguish:

- **B1 — the extractor projects.** The presupposition prompt hints at
  time/simultaneity as one of four classes to look for, and the model
  supplied `instant across whole medium` for Maxwell because it was
  primed to. If B1 is correct, DCR1e's five T1 propositions are
  contaminated in a way that a wider matcher cannot detect.

- **B2 — the placebo was invalid.** Absolute simultaneity is not first
  presupposed in electrodynamics of the 1880s. It is presupposed by any
  classical field theory that quantifies over "the whole medium at an
  instant" — which Maxwell's electromagnetic theory does from at least
  1865. If B2 is correct, the DCR1c/d 1880 cut never was a real control
  for T1, and its silence in DCR1c/d was a matcher artifact of a different
  kind: DCR1c's stated-commitment extractor simply did not surface the
  field-theoretic construction, while DCR1e's presupposition extractor did.

DCR1f does not distinguish B1 and B2. And R2's 32.5% failure means
`target_v4` is not the right instrument to force the distinction with
either — its calibration is fitted narrowly and does not generalise to
the register of held-out philosophical prose. Any DCR1g redraft that
narrows the pattern to remove the Maxwell hit will also narrow it further
away from the held-out T1 assertions, worsening R2 as it improves R5.

The instrument reached its precision-recall wall.

## 4. What DCR1f establishes about the target

Read together, the five DCR1e T1-content propositions + the DCR1d Newton
positive control + the R2 held-out register **span a class**, not a
point:

| register | example | source |
|---|---|---|
| explicit metaphysical assertion | *"Absolute, true, and mathematical time… flows equably without regard to anything external"* | Newton 1687 |
| explicit mathematical operation | *"There is a common time t in which the position of an electron in the moving medium can be compared to its position in the medium at rest at time t minus vx over c squared"* | Larmor 1900 ch11 |
| explicit definite-and-independent duration | *"The time of journey of light… is perfectly definite and independent of the motion of the material"* | Lodge 1897 |
| explicit "regarded as simultaneous" convention | *"…the two events are regarded as simultaneous"* | Poincaré 1898 |
| field-theoretic "instant across whole X" | *"There is an instant at which the amount of energy in the whole medium…"* | Maxwell 1865 |
| literary "duration is intrinsic" register | *"The duration between two events is a quantity intrinsic to those events"* | (held-out) |

Each is a phrasing of absolute simultaneity. Each requires different
matcher constructions to fire on. **DCR1c/d's binary "T1 present at cut
vs absent at cut" question presupposed the target was a single
recognisable pattern; DCR1f shows the target is a spectrum.**

This is a real programmatic finding. It says: nomination over an extracted
proposition set as currently designed cannot ask *"was the T1 commitment
in the corpus?"* because T1 does not have a single realisation to look
for. The framework has to be re-cast — either at the extractor (produce a
single canonical `T1_assertion` per document if any) or at the
nominator (rank by *class* rather than by *individual proposition*).

## 5. What DCR1f does not license

- Post-hoc tightening of `target_v4` to remove the R5 hit. Preregistration
  §5 forbids it.
- Post-hoc widening of `target_v4` to fix R2. Same reason.
- Reversing DCR1c's or DCR1d's published verdicts. Their reproduction
  commands still produce their published numbers; DCR1f adds a new
  matcher without editing any prior module.
- A general claim about which of B1 vs B2 is correct. That is genuinely
  open and requires a different experiment shape.

## 6. Where the ceiling actually sits

The DCR1c–DCR1f arc has now established six things worth keeping:

1. A real 15-document pre-1905 electrodynamics corpus, chrome-stripped
   structurally, checksummed, provenance-cleared (DCR1).
2. Stated-commitment extraction produces T2 (privileged frame) and T3
   (local time) hits at the 1904 cut, silent at the 1880 placebo — the
   COGR-style candidate-selection circularity is real and here it is
   absent (DCR1c).
3. The T1 matcher fires on the sentence it was written for (DCR1d).
4. A presupposition-inferring extractor surfaces T1 content across the
   corpus (DCR1e) at a rate the stated-commitment extractor did not.
5. A matcher successor calibrated to the DCR1e content fires on 7 of the
   consensus propositions at the target cut, but also fires at the deep
   placebo, and does not generalise to a wider held-out register (DCR1f).
6. The T1 target is not a single realisable proposition. It is a
   spectrum of related field-theoretic, mathematical, and metaphysical
   assertions. Any framework that operationalises T1 as "one thing" is
   asking a question the target does not answer.

## 7. The programmatic conclusion

**Deletion-repair nomination as currently designed cannot cleanly answer
whether Einstein's deletion is reachable from the pre-1905 corpus.** Not
because the corpus does not contain the content — it does, in multiple
registers — but because the operationalisation of "the deletion" as a
matcher pattern requires committing to one realisation of what is
actually a spectrum.

Two ways forward, both requiring a different next experiment:

- **DCR2a — nominate by class, not by proposition.** Modify the
  nominator to score deletion candidates by *facet coverage* rather than
  by *specific-proposition rank*. The load-bearing test becomes whether
  the class {absolute-simultaneity assertions surfaced by any of the
  DCR1e T1-content phrasings} is ranked highly by the nominator's
  execution-free scoring.

- **DR5-shape theorem.** Prove or characterise the class of deletions
  that admit multiple non-equivalent surface realisations, and show
  formally what a proposition-ranked nominator can and cannot do with
  them. This is the DR2-precedent path: convert an empirical wall into a
  structural result.

Either is a paper. Neither is a "wider matcher" of the DCR1f kind. The
DCR1c–DCR1f arc has reached the point where the honest answer is *the
question needs to be restated*, and the next paper should restate it.
