# Information-Limited Discovery

This directory contains the calibrated V0 paper and its prior-art ledger.

- `paper.md` is the publication source.
- `source_notes.md` records the novelty boundary and primary-source links.
- `paper.pdf` is the rendered shareable copy.
- `scripts/build_information_limited_discovery_pdf.py` rebuilds the PDF and
  mirrors it into `papers/pdf/` and the local Metaphysics archive when present.

The paper treats Relative Identifiability as its standard mathematical kernel.
Its new claim is limited to the obstruction-certificate benchmark contract and
the deterministic finite V0 implementation.
