# DCR1f — Does a Presupposition-Calibrated Matcher Fire on What DCR1e Extracted?

**Package:** `experiments/date_cut_retrodiction/` (matcher successor)
**Predecessor:** DCR1e (NO_GO strict, but extractor surfaced T1 content in 5 documents that `target_v3` rejected)
**Human director:** Jawaun Brown
**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Written:** BEFORE `target_v4.py` is drafted, BEFORE the held-out validation set is generated, BEFORE any scoring runs.

## 0. The question DCR1f is designed to answer

DCR1e ran a presupposition-inferring extractor over the DCR1c corpus + Newton.
It surfaced content of the T1 (absolute simultaneity) form in five distinct
documents — Larmor 1900 ch11, Lodge 1897, Maxwell 1865 pt1, Poincaré 1898
(×2) — and `target_v3` fired on none of them. Two readings remained
undecided:

- **Reading A** — matcher calibration was too narrow. Fix the matcher, the
  DCR1c/d T1 absence becomes an instrument artifact, DCR2 becomes real.
- **Reading B** — the extractor was projecting. The presupposition prompt
  hints at what to look for and the model supplied T1-shaped content in
  documents whose arguments do not actually require it.

DCR1f is a single experiment that discriminates them.

## 1. What is added, and what is untouched

**Added, in this order:**

1. `DCR1F_PREREGISTRATION.md` (this file), before anything else.
2. **The held-out validation set**, generated by *sandboxed subagents that
   have not seen DCR1e outputs and have not seen `target_v3`*. Two
   independent subagents each produce (i) 20 sentences in
   nineteenth/early-twentieth-century scientific-philosophical prose style
   that assert or presuppose absolute simultaneity, and (ii) 20 sentences
   asserting relativity of simultaneity. The **union** of both agents'
   sets is the held-out set, committed to
   `experiments/date_cut_retrodiction/heldout_simultaneity_validation.json`.
3. `target_v4.py` — a new matcher module, drafted by linguistic analysis of
   what presuppositional simultaneity phrasings look like in scientific
   prose, WITHOUT the author reading the held-out set produced in step 2.
   The `target_v3` module is not edited; every DCR1a/b/c/d/e reproduction
   command continues to produce byte-identical numbers.
4. `run_dcr1f.py` — validates `target_v4` against the held-out set (R2/R3),
   then scores it against the DCR1e consensus at each cut (R4/R5), then
   sanity-checks Newton (R6).

**Not changed:** every prior matcher, extraction module, source list, cut
list, prompt, runner. Sixteen regression tests already lock the shape of
the corpus, the residue relational definition, the placebo silence, the
matcher patterns for v2/v3, the consensus discipline, and the DCR1e
recognizer gap.

## 2. Gates

Six gates, all must decide GO for the overall verdict to be GO.

- **R1 — held-out set is well-formed.** Each subagent produced at least 15
  T1-positive sentences and 15 T1-negative sentences. All sentences are in
  scientific-prose register (not aphorisms, not fragments), independently
  meaningful, and use no author names or twentieth-century technical terms
  ("Einstein", "Lorentz transformation", "spacetime interval").
- **R2 — T1-positive accuracy.** `target_v4` fires on **at least 70%** of the
  T1-positive held-out sentences. Threshold set here, before drafting
  `target_v4`. Chosen as roughly the accuracy `target_v3` achieved on
  DCR1c's explicit validation phrasings; a matcher that cannot match its
  predecessor's ability on the equivalent register is not an improvement.
- **R3 — T1-negative rejection.** `target_v4` rejects **at least 85%** of the
  T1-negative (relativity-of-simultaneity) sentences. Higher than R2 because
  false positives on the negative set are exactly the failure mode Reading B
  is worried about — a matcher that fires on the *denial* of absolute
  simultaneity does not distinguish content from surface form.
- **R4 — T1 fires at DCR1e 1897 cut.** Under `target_v4`, `match_facets_v4`
  applied to the DCR1e consensus at the 1897 cut produces at least one
  T1 hit. This is the Reading-A test.
- **R5 — T1 silent at DCR1e 1880 cut.** Under `target_v4`, the same at 1880
  produces zero T1 hits. This is the Reading-B test. **Any T1 hit at 1880
  is evidence the extractor is projecting, and per the decision table
  below the paper does not license Reading A regardless of R2/R3/R4.**
- **R6 — Newton sanity.** `target_v4` fires on Newton's *"Absolute time…
  flows equably"* as before. Preserves the DCR1d positive control.

## 4. What DCR1f does not test

- Whether DCR2 works on the enriched consensus. That is DCR2's question.
- Whether target_v4 handles T2 and T3 as well as target_v3. DCR1f is
  strictly a T1-matcher successor; T2/T3 alternatives are copied over
  unchanged.
- Whether the framework can find deletions in other domains. This whole
  arc is the electrodynamics test.

## 5. Single-shot commitment

One drafting of `target_v4`, one validation, one scoring, one adjudication
of any 1897 hit. Verdict written to `results/dcr1f_verdict.json`. No
replay knobs.

**If R5 fails, do not remove the 1880 hits by tightening the matcher.**
That would be exactly the manipulation the placebo is there to detect.
Report R5 NO_GO and license Reading B honestly.

**If R2/R3 fails, do not tune target_v4 to the held-out set and re-score.**
That collapses the validation into curve-fitting. Report R2/R3 NO_GO and
redraft the matcher with a *different* linguistic hypothesis — same
held-out set, new attempt, single shot again.
