03 The boundary

      
A receipt is useful because it says where it stops.
Bio Claim Firewall is designed for the moment before a scientific-diligence memo hardens an assertion into a decision. It makes the evidence contract inspectable, not invisible.
Talk through a design-partner workflow →
01World-bound
Selecting a world is explicit. No parser or prompt can switch it.
02Fail-closed
Missing, corrupt, stale, or out-of-scope evidence cannot pass.
03Reproducible
The same structured claim and world digest produce the same receipt.

      
Read the limitations
This demo does not provide clinical decision support, investment advice, efficacy claims, or a general public checking API. It uses compact sanitized fixtures; source acquisition and model-assisted parsing happen offline. A public badge means “consistent with this declared evidence contract,” nothing more.
