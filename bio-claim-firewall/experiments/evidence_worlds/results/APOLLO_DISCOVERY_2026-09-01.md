# Private design-partner discovery — 2026-09-01

## Decision

Apollo discovery found enough account and role coverage to justify manual
design-partner qualification, but it does not prove demand. No outreach was
performed.

## Bounded method

- Queried Apollo company search once for each of three preregistered wedges:
  clinical-trial diligence, target validation, and virtual-cell platforms.
- Retained five organization candidates per wedge in an ignored, owner-only
  local directory.
- Queried Apollo people search for role categories only. Email and phone
  enrichment, sequences, messaging, and CRM mutation were not used.
- Published only the aggregate companion JSON. It contains no company list,
  person identity, contact information, provider identifiers, response body,
  or credential.

## Aggregate outcome

| Measure | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Candidate organizations | 15 |
| Candidates with a domain | 15 |
| Organizations checked for roles | 15 |
| Role candidates returned | 25 |
| Outreach sent | 0 |

The returned roles included the intended scientific and computational buyer
categories. Provider matching also returned possible vendors, publishers, and
records that may be stale after acquisitions, so every account still requires
manual public-source qualification before contact.

## Safe next step

After the three-world pilot gates pass, manually validate the first five
accounts against current official company pages. Seek design-partner calls
with scientific-diligence, computational-biology, translational-informatics,
or platform leaders. Do not purchase contact data or send automated outreach
until the pilot workflow and problem statement are confirmed in interviews.
